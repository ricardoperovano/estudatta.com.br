02What the service is — and what it is not

Estudatta is a study and practice planner. You set a goal, a time target per day, the days of the week and, if you like, subjects, topics, materials and tasks. The service organizes the plan, logs the sessions (by timer or manual entry), calculates the day’s balance and turns what was missed into time to make up, by the rule you choose.

The service does not:

  • teach languages or offer exam content, questions, lessons or grading;

The results of your studying depend on your material, your method and you.
