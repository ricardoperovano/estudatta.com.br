Start session

“I wanted to study an hour a day, but I don’t know how much I studied, how much is still owed or how to get back on track.”

That is what the app is for. It doesn’t teach and it doesn’t measure fluency: it organizes the studying you already do with courses, books, PDFs, videos and exercises — and shows you, every day, the commitment, what got done and the next step.

How it works
01

Set the goal and the syllabus

Goal, minutes per day, days of the week and deadline. Subjects, topics, materials and pages live in one place; the syllabus can be imported from text, CSV or PDF.

02

Plan the week

Preferred times, weekly goals for questions and pages, and the day’s reviews. The app suggests the next subject; you decide.

03

Log it with the timer and Tatá

Pick the session type, study with the timer and note questions done and correct answers. Forgot to open the app? Manual logging takes care of it. Works offline.

04

Track progress and achievements

Time, accuracy per subject, mock exams and syllabus covered. A day with nothing logged becomes time to make up, and getting back on track counts as an achievement too.

FAQ

What the app does — and what it doesn’t

Does the app teach languages or include exam content?

No. It organizes the studying you do with courses, books, videos and classes, and tracks the time spent on each part.

What happens when I don’t log a day?

The goal’s time becomes owed time, following the rule you chose: spread it over the next days, accumulate it and decide later, or just be notified. You can log it later if you studied and forgot.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sessions are saved on the device and sync when you are back online. Signing in, paying and importing files need a connection.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It opens in the browser and can be added to your phone’s or computer’s home screen as an app.

If I break my streak, do I lose achievements?

No. Achievements, XP and level stay with you. Only the streak count restarts, and there is no leaderboard: the comparison is with yourself.

Can I turn Tatá off?

Yes. In Preferences, inside the app, you can hide the mascot whenever you like.

Does the report tell me whether I am learning?

No. It shows consistency, time and accuracy on the questions you log. Learning is measured in your own exercises, exams and conversations.
