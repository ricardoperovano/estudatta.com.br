FAQ

Questions from students preparing for entrance exams

Does the app have video lessons, handouts or exam questions?

No. It organizes the studying you already do with your prep course, books, PDFs and the video lessons you choose, and tracks time per area and subject. Questions and mock exams done elsewhere are logged here, with the correct answers.

How do I build a study schedule for the exam?

Create the goal with the exam date, the minutes per day and the days of the week. Add the areas (Languages, Humanities, Natural Sciences, Mathematics and Essay) as subjects and the themes as topics. Every day the app shows the goal, what has been logged and the next step.

Can I study several subjects on the same day?

Yes. The time goal belongs to the objective; each session is logged under a topic. At the end of the day you see the total and the split per area.

How do I track mock exams per area?

Log each mock exam with correct answers per area or subject. The history shows the progress from one mock exam to the next and where it is worth reinforcing.

What about the essay?

Add Essay as a subject and log writing and correction sessions. If you like, use tasks for “write one essay a week”: they show up in the daily plan.

I lost a week. Now what?

The missing time becomes owed time, by the rule you chose: spread it over the next days, accumulate it and decide later, or just be notified. In school test weeks, use a planned break so no debt builds up.

Does the app raise my score?

We don’t promise that. The app shows consistency, time per area, accuracy and progress in mock exams. Your score depends on many things it doesn’t measure.

Does it work for entrance exams like Fuvest, Unicamp and UFRGS?

Yes. Each exam can be a goal with its own date and syllabus, or you use a single goal with the subjects they share. The Free plan has one active goal; Essential, up to three.
