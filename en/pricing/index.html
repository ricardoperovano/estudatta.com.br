No fine print

What holds on any plan

History and export never depend on a subscription

Everything you logged stays visible and exportable on any plan, including after cancelling. The data is yours.

Cancellation without hurdles

You cancel on the plans screen, inside the app. No phone call, no retention form. Access to the paid plan runs until the end of the period already paid.

No per-minute charges

The price doesn’t change with study time, number of sessions or reminders. Studying more doesn’t cost more.

Billing questions

Billing, cancellation and your data

Do I need a card to start?

No. The free account doesn’t ask for a payment method. You only enter payment details if you decide to subscribe to a paid plan.

How much do the paid plans cost?

Essential: R$ 9.90 a month or R$ 94.80 a year. Complete: R$ 19.90 a month or R$ 190.80 a year (prices in Brazilian reais). The prices on this page come from the service catalog; what counts is what appears on the app’s plans screen at the time of subscribing.

When is the paid plan unlocked after payment?

When the payment provider confirms the charge. Returning from the payment page alone doesn’t unlock the plan; confirmation usually arrives within moments and the app updates by itself.

How do I cancel? What happens next?

On the app’s plans screen, in a few taps. You keep the paid plan until the end of the period already paid and then return to Free. Nothing is deleted.

If I return to Free with several goals, do I lose any?

No. Nothing is deleted. If there are more active goals than Free allows, the most recent stay active and the others are paused until you choose. History, materials and export remain available.

Are there charges per use, per minute or per reminder?

No. The plan has a fixed price per month or per year, whatever is in the catalog.

Are there discounts, coupons or promotions?

The only discount is the yearly plan, which is 20% cheaper than 12 monthly payments. There are no coupons or limited-time promotional prices.
