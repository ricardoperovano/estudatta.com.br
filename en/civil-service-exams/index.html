Syllabus import

Text, CSV or PDF — always with your review

01

Paste, upload the spreadsheet or the PDF

Text pasted from the notice, a CSV file (there is a template to download) or the notice’s own PDF.

02

Review the preview

The app drafts a proposal of subjects and topics. You rename, move, merge or delete whatever doesn’t make sense.

03

Confirm

Nothing enters your plan before you confirm. Changed your mind? Just cancel the import.

A scanned PDF with no selectable text is not read: the import warns you and you continue with pasted text or CSV. On the Essential and Complete plans, AI can suggest the subject structure from the notice; it only runs when you ask, and the suggestion also goes through your review.

FAQ

Questions from exam candidates

Does the app have exam content, lessons or questions?

No. It has no video lessons, handouts or question bank. It organizes the studying you do with your course, your PDFs and your books, and tracks the time spent on each subject. Questions you solve elsewhere can be logged in the session, with the correct answers.

How do automatic reviews work?

After a session on a topic, the app schedules reviews at 1, 7 and 30 days. You can change these intervals, and the day’s reviews show up in today’s plan.

Can I log mock exams from other platforms?

Yes. Enter the mock exam and the correct answers per subject; the app keeps the history and shows the progress.

Does the app improve my chances of passing?

We don’t promise that. The app shows commitment, time done and owed time per subject. Passing depends on many things it neither measures nor controls.

How does the syllabus import work?

You paste the notice text, upload a CSV or the PDF. The app proposes a structure of subjects and topics and you review it before confirming. Text and CSV are ready instantly; the PDF is processed next and notifies you when the preview is available.

What if the notice PDF is scanned, with no selectable text?

The import fails with a clear warning instead of making up a structure. In that case, paste the text manually or use the CSV template.

Can I study several subjects on the same day?

Yes. The time goal belongs to the objective; each session is logged under a topic. At the end of the day you see the total and the split per subject.

What happens to the plan if the exam is postponed or the syllabus changes?

You change the deadline and edit the subject tree. Sessions already logged stay in the history and reports.

Can I track more than one exam at a time?

Each exam is a goal. The Free plan has one active goal, Essential up to three and Complete has no limit. Details are on the plans page.
